How to play

Find the words that fit today's theme.

  • Drag across touching letters, or tap them one at a time, to spell a word.
  • Tap the last letter again (or press Enter) to submit it.
  • Theme words turn green.
  • One special word stretches right across the board and describes the whole theme. It turns yellow.
  • Every letter on the board belongs to exactly one word.
  • Find any other real word to charge up a hint.
