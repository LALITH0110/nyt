How to play

Make as many words as you can from seven letters.

  • Words must be at least four letters long.
  • Every word must include the centre letter.
  • Letters can be reused as often as you like.
  • Four-letter words score 1 point. Longer words score one point per letter.
  • A pangram uses all seven letters and scores 7 bonus points.
  • Type on your keyboard or tap the hexagons.
