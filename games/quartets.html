How to play

Sort sixteen words into four secret groups.

  • Pick four words you think belong together, then hit Submit.
  • Get it right and the group locks in, colour-coded by difficulty.
  • Yellow is the gentlest group; purple is the sneakiest.
  • If three of your four are correct, you'll be told you're one away.
  • Four wrong guesses and the answers are revealed.
