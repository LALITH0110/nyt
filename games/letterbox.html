Letter Boxed

Words: 0 Letters left: 12 Target: 2 words

How to play

Use all twelve letters in as few words as you can.

  • Make a word by clicking letters, or just type.
  • Two letters in a row may never come from the same side of the square.
  • Each new word must start with the letter the last word ended on.
  • Letters can be used more than once.
  • You win when every one of the twelve letters has been used at least once.
